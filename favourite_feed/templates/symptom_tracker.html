What seems to affect my symptoms?

My personal insights

Automatic summary of the last 30 symptom days for exercise (same day to three days later), and 14 days either side of up to five recent ongoing changes within 90 days. These patterns do not establish causes.

Exercise associations

Only explicitly recorded exercise and rest days are compared. Other activity and changes may influence later symptoms.

Exercise & recovery

Duration bands are descriptive, not exercise targets. Weekly frequency requires seven explicitly recorded activity or rest days; recovery comparisons require two consecutive recorded days. Rolling weeks overlap. Other activity, symptoms and medication changes may influence results.

Menstrual cycle associations

Period starts use explicitly entered start dates or a recorded period immediately following a recorded no-bleeding day. Other comparisons require an explicit no-bleeding answer. The week after means after the start, not the end. Overlapping windows are excluded; unknown bleeding days are excluded. No ovulation or hormone phase is inferred.

Ongoing change comparisons

Compare saved symptom ratings before and after a dated change. These are associations, not proof of benefit or harm.

Change day excluded. Missing answers are not zero. At least five ratings on each side are needed to label a difference; a change of 0.5 points is a descriptive threshold, not a clinical or statistical test.
